# Search research for the BUSRA blog

Reviewed on 27 November 2026 for homes, shops and offices in Seri Kembangan and the Klang Valley.

## What Google users ask

Google Autocomplete was queried with the Malaysia country setting and Malay interface (`gl=my`, `hl=ms`). Returned suggestions included **pintu sliding susah dibuka**, **pintu sliding lipat**, **tingkap aluminium casement**, **harga kabinet dapur aluminium sekaki**, **office glass partition price per sq ft**, **shower screen sliding door** and **shower screen swing door**. Some results named Singapore or unrelated products. The editorial selection favours problems BUSRA can address locally. These observations show wording and intent. They do not establish monthly volume, conversion rate or a ranking of the ten most popular questions. ## Where the terms appear

Each topic has a Malay and an English article, a direct answer near the top, an illustrated checklist or comparison, a relevant service link and a quotation link. The listing links to all ten topics. Each detail page has its own title, description, social image, language alternates, breadcrumbs and BlogPosting structured data. Internal URLs keep the existing clean trailing-slash format.

After the custom domain is connected, compare Search Console impressions, clicks and queries by language and article against quotation enquiries. Use that first-party evidence to decide which topics deserve expansion.
