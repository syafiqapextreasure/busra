# BUSRA page-by-page design audit

Each row compares the approved desktop mockup with the Malay browser renders at 1440 px desktop, 768 px tablet and 390 px phone. The separate visual review package includes the reference and all three renders for each page.

| Page | Matched in the browser | Corrections and retained differences |
|---|---|---|
| 00 · Home | Split hero with the glazing inset, three benefit icons, six service cards, navy three-step band and quote panel. | The form adds a required area/postcode, visible field labels and a privacy link. A directory link exposes the two additional services. The mobile hero and quote panel stack. |
| 01 · About | Glazing hero, three customer and work categories, four-step approach beside a window photo, navy credentials strip and local-area panel. | The desktop hero height was increased. The approach and location panels stack on phones. The credentials strip invites registration enquiries. |
| 02 · Services | Split lead, filter buttons, eight photo cards, three-step band and assistance CTA. | Desktop card photos were made taller and the last two cards span half the row each. The route uses a distinct patio hero. The tablet grid keeps three columns and the phone grid uses two. |
| 03 · Aluminium doors | Black folding-door hero, three door options, frame-track inset, two FAQ rows and the window and shopfront links. | The hero, option shots and track inset were given more height at desktop. On phones, the CTA sits below the two related cards. |
| 04 · Aluminium windows | Casement-window hero, casement/sliding/custom options, hardware inset and links to doors and glass. | The shared image proportions were corrected. The Malay title wraps at some desktop and tablet widths, and the options use two columns on phones. |
| 05 · Shower screens | Glazed shower hero, fixed/hinged/corner option images, door fitting inset and related mirror and window cards. | The option photos and fitting inset were enlarged for desktop. The fitting photo moves below the considerations on phones. |
| 06 · Shopfront glass | Retail façade hero, frontage/door/layout options, track detail and related wall glazing and doors. | The desktop crops were made taller. Phone placement moves the track detail below the considerations and the CTA below related cards. |
| 07 · Wall glazing | Low-rise glazed façade, panel and frame options, technical-considerations inset and shopfront and construction links. | The taller desktop façade crop restores more of the building. The technical-assessment note remains beside the checklist on desktop and above the detail image on phones. |
| 08 · Glass and mirrors | Vanity-mirror hero, three glass and mirror option photos, edge detail and related shower and shopfront cards. | Desktop photo heights were increased to bring the hero, options and detail into closer proportion with the reference. The consideration columns stack on phones. |
| 09 · Aluminium cabinets | Kitchen hero, base/wall/layout options, cabinet-edge close-up and related glass and windows. | The option and detail cards gained height on desktop. The title uses two lines where width requires it. |
| 10 · Construction | Building-site hero, three icon cards, enquiry checklist, site-detail photo and construction-specific process band. | The desktop site photos and type sizes were enlarged. The first scope question starts expanded. The scope cards and checklist reflow on phones. |
| 11 · Projects | Design-example badge, five category filters, six photo cards and a design-enquiry CTA. | The desktop and tablet gallery uses two columns and the phone uses one. The folding-door card opens the design case page. The other five cards open their related service pages. |
| 12 · Project detail | Full-width folding-door photo, three thumbnails, split design narrative and summary, plus navy CTA and related service cards. | The desktop CTA band was shortened. Thumbnails change the main photo, which opens in a keyboard-dismissible dialog. The design summary stacks on phones. |
| 13 · FAQ | Split introduction, four category links, grouped questions with the reference answers expanded and navy contact panel. | Desktop answer spacing was increased. The route uses a distinct window-detail hero. On phones, the category menu sits above the questions. |
| 14 · Contact | Three methods, address beside the illustrated map, area guidance, enquiry form and visit note. | Corrected a card style that clipped the phone and WhatsApp buttons, then recaptured all widths. The photo hero uses a distinct commercial façade. The map search uses the supplied address while the illustration remains labelled. |
| 15 · Request a quote | Intro, grouped fields, dashed photo area and next-steps sidebar. | The route uses a workshop-detail hero. Desktop fields were enlarged and the sidebar follows the form on phones. Upload type and selected filenames are checked in the browser. |
| 16 · Quote confirmation | Centred check, reference strip, three next steps and two linked suggestion cards in the successful state. | Captures use a real localhost test reference in place of the mockup placeholder. A direct visit without the browser receipt shows a neutral state. The reference wraps on narrow screens. |
| 17 · Privacy | Draft marker, contents panel, six numbered sections and a separate analytics section. | Desktop article spacing was increased and the contents panel moves above the article on phones. The draft now describes actual Cloudflare storage, staff access and aggregate metrics. Retention remains pending company review. |
