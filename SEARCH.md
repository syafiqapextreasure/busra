# BUSRA local search brief

The priority phrase for the Malay homepage is **pakar aluminium dan kaca Seri Kembangan**. Its English counterpart is **aluminium and glass specialist Seri Kembangan**. Both describe the services shown on the posters and the location a customer can verify. These priorities are qualitative, based on service fit and observed local competitors. Search volume and ranking have not been measured.

Soliq has a Seri Kembangan showroom and offers sliding doors, shower screens and windows. Aik Lian has a sliding-door page aimed specifically at Seri Kembangan, Serdang and Balakong. They compete for overlapping searches. BUSRA’s clearest angle is the combination of domestic and shopfront work, with local contact information, project options and a quote form in both languages. [Soliq](https://soliq.com.my/contact-us/) · [Aik Lian Marketing](https://www.aiklian.com.my/showproducts/productid/6456619/cid/487696/telescopicstyle-sliding-door-suppliers-in-seri-kembangan-serdang-balakong/)

Each service has its own crawlable address, heading, service options, relevant questions and enquiry link. Malay and English versions link to each other. The homepage, service pages, and FAQ answer practical questions that customers or answer engines can quote. Business information and schema should be updated together when the address, contact details or service scope change.

After connecting the final domain, submit its sitemap in Search Console. Compare actual queries, enquiry submissions and contact clicks by landing page before changing keyword priorities. No customer identifiers are needed for those measurements.
