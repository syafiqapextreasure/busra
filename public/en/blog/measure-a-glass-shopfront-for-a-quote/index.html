What should you measure for a shopfront glass quote?

BUSRA team Share
27 Nov 2026

Sketch the frontage from the walkway

A front-on photo, rough dimensions and the door swing are more useful than glass area alone. Show how customers approach the entrance too.

Draw a rectangle for the whole opening, then mark fixed panels and doors. State whether you used millimetres or feet. Take one wide photo and a side view that shows columns, steps or an existing shutter. These rough measurements support an estimate. The installer needs final site measurements before cutting glass.

Details that change the price

Share requirements for locks, handles, privacy film and removal of the previous frontage. For a mall unit, obtain management’s rules about after-hours work. Ask for separate lines for fixed panels, the door set and site work so you can review alternatives.
