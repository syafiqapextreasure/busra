How do you choose a bathroom mirror size?

BUSRA team Share

Test the size from where you stand

Use a paper template to test sightlines, width and height before ordering a custom-cut mirror.

Mark the proposed outline with tape appropriate for the wall finish. Have the shortest and tallest users stand at the basin. Do both eye lines fall inside the outline? Consider the reflection from the doorway too. A wall-to-wall mirror can reflect the toilet or a bright light directly into view.

Prepare a photo for the order

Take a front-on photo with the template still in place. Share the desired dimensions, wall finish and socket positions. For a mirror between two walls, leave final cutting dimensions and the choice of adhesive or support to the installer. Slight variation in the tiling can affect the fit.
