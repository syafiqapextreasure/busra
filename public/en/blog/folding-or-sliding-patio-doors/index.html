Folding or sliding doors for a patio?

BUSRA team Share
27 Nov 2026

Leave somewhere for the panels to park

Compare the clear passage when the door is open. Two frames of equal width can give very different usable openings.

If a dining table sits beside the patio, folded panels can clash with a chair. A sliding door runs parallel to the wall, but its stacked panels occupy part of the opening. Tape the parked position on the floor, then pull a chair out or walk through carrying a tray.

Choose for everyday use

For everyday access, focus on the leaf you use most and where its handle ends up. If you often open the whole space for guests, ask for an all-panels-open drawing. If combining glass with security mesh, request the clear width with both systems open.
