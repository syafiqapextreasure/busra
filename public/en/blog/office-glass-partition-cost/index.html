What affects the cost of an office glass partition?

BUSRA team Share
27 Nov 2026

Start with a simple elevation

A price per square foot only helps when both quotes cover comparable glass, doors, hardware and installation.

Draw the wall as you would see it standing in front of it. Mark its width, height, door and fixed panels. A 4 m wide opening at 2.7 m high has a gross area of 10.8 m². A door within that opening is already in the gross measurement. Ask how the supplier deducts or prices the door so you don’t count it twice.

Compare totals for the same scope

For a Klang Valley office, include the floor level, permitted working hours and goods-lift access. If speech privacy matters, ask for a specification covering the entire system including the door. Put exclusions beside each total. A lower figure may leave out frosting or hardware.
