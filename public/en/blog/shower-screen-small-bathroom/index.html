Which shower screen fits a small bathroom?

BUSRA team Share

Measure the entrance first

Map the toilet, basin and shower head. The right screen gives you a comfortable entrance and helps contain spray.

Measure after tiling. On a dry floor, mark the end of the proposed screen with tape and try your usual step into the shower. For a hinged door, mark the swing arc and check it against the toilet and basin. Also check where you would stand to reach a towel afterwards.

Questions before ordering

Share the width, depth, spray direction and a photo of the access route. Ask the installer to check support positions, glass specification and the finished floor on site. If stepping through the taped opening feels awkward, revise the layout before the glass size is final.
