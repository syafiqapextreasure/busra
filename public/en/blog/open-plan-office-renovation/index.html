From an empty office to an open workspace

BUSRA team Share
27 Nov 2026

Before work begins

This illustrated office plan shows how to divide an open floor while preserving window light and a clear route to each desk.

In this illustrated layout, the windows sit at the far end. Putting a solid meeting room wall across them would leave the middle desks dependent on artificial light. Sketch two arrangements. On each, trace a visitor’s route to the meeting room and a staff member’s route to a desk. Mark where those paths cross.

The finished layout

Glazed rooms give meetings a defined space while retaining a view through to the windows. A frosted band adds visual privacy. Before choosing finishes, ask how sound can travel through the door and above the ceiling. Tape out the door position in the empty room and walk the proposed route.
