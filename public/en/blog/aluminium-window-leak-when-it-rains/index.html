Aluminium window leaking in rain? Trace the first wet spot

BUSRA team Share
27 Nov 2026

Observe from inside during wet weather

The puddle may be far from the entry point. Note the first wet spot and the weather conditions before arranging an inspection.

When safe, dry the inside sill and photograph the first place water reappears. Do droplets cover the indoor glass on an ordinary morning, or is there one trail at the frame edge only during wind-driven rain? That distinction helps an installer investigate condensation, track drainage or the wall joint.

When to arrange a site check

If water repeatedly enters the room or paint starts to blister, send before-and-after rain photos, the window type and the floor level. Avoid sealing drainage openings or leaning outside. Photos establish the pattern, but a site inspection determines the cause.
