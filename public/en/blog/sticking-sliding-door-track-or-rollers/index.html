Sliding door hard to move? Check the track and rollers

BUSRA team Share

Find the pattern before ordering parts

When a sliding door sticks, notice where it catches. Grit in one spot can feel different from a roller that drags along the whole track.

With the panel still on its track, open it slowly from inside. If it catches in the same spot, look for a pebble or packed dirt. Vacuum the accessible track and wipe it with a damp cloth. Try again once dry. If it grinds along the full travel, record the sound for the installer.

What the installer needs

Send a short side-on video, a photo of the lower track and the number of panels. Mention whether the problem followed dusty work or developed gradually. Leave glass-panel removal and roller adjustments to the installer. Keep the opening clear if the panel feels loose.
