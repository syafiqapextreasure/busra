1. Information You Share

Your name, phone number, email if provided, project location, project summary and any uploaded photos.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information to manage enquiries, understand project requirements and communicate about quotes.

3. Access to Information

Authorised BUSRA staff can review enquiries and download photos. When you upload photos for WhatsApp, anyone who receives a photo link can view that image for 7 days. This website uses Cloudflare D1 and private Cloudflare Workers KV storage. Staff alerts, when enabled, contain only the enquiry reference.

4. Retention

The notice will set out retention periods and deletion methods after the company confirms its policy.
